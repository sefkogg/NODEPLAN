Quick fixes

Can't move around in 2D?

Move the pointer over the floor plan. Drag an empty part of the canvas to pan. Use Ctrl/Cmd + scroll to zoom, or Fit View to recenter.

CableTrace isn't showing connections?

Add devices and cables in NetPlan first, then switch to CableTrace and select Refresh. Manual connections can also be added in CableTrace.

Something looks missing or blank?

Switch to NetPlan, use Fit View, then return to your workspace. Try resizing the browser window. Export your project before clearing browser data.

Changes aren't being saved?

Browser storage can be blocked in private browsing or restricted environments. Use the project's export/download option to keep a backup.

Report an issue

Describe what happened and copy or download a report to share. This offline app cannot submit reports automatically.

About NODEPLAN

NetPlan is the red workspace for spatial network planning and 2D/3D layouts.

CableTrace is the blue workspace for documenting ports, cables, and physical connections.

Your projects stay on this device unless you export them. Diagnostics do not contact a server.
